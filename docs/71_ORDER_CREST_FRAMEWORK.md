### 6.1 The Singularity Myth

In classical general relativity, certain solutions of the field equations produce divergent (infinite) density values. These were named "singularities" and treated as physical objects. This is an error.

**The Taurus position:** A divergence in a field equation is a signal that the framework has exceeded its domain of validity. The equation is failing, not the universe. Einstein understood this. The singularity myth was imposed on his equations by later interpreters who mistook mathematical limit behaviour for physical prediction.

**Correction:** At the Crest boundary, the framework undergoes a *Disruption* — a state transition to a new structural regime described by the next order of the magnitude ladder. There is no infinite density. There is a Crest closure event at finite scale.

### 6.2 Disruption vs. Collapse

| Classical Term | Classical Meaning | Taurus Replacement | Reason |
|---------------|-------------------|--------------------|--------|
| Collapse | Irreversible terminal failure | Disruption (D) | Transitions are real; terminations are not |
| Singularity | Infinite density point | Crest boundary | Finite geometry, bounded disruption |
| Black hole interior | Singularity + event horizon | Disruption zone | State transition, not physical terminus |

Collapse implies termination. Disruption implies transformation. The operator hierarchy applies through the Disruption event and continues to Absorption and beyond.

### 6.3 Crest vs. Infinity

The Apex scale (10³⁶ m) is not infinite. It is the **Terminal Geometry** — the outer Crest boundary of the observable and structural universe. The Taurus framework does not extrapolate beyond it because there is no validated physical ladder beyond L71.

Infinity in classical physics serves as a placeholder for "we don't know the boundary." Taurus replaces that placeholder with a finite Crest at L71 and a Terminal Invariant (the white center point in the Apex Seal) that remains constant across all scales.

### 6.4 Absolute Zero as Origin Crest

In thermodynamics, absolute zero (0 K) is typically treated as an unreachable lower bound — a limit, not a boundary. In the Taurus framework, **Absolute Zero is the Origin Crest**: L0, the Planck length, the physical lower boundary of the magnitude ladder.

It is not zero-valued in the sense of absence. It is the **minimum nonzero structural configuration** — the first stable quantum state above the vacuum. The Planck length is not empty; it is the floor of the ladder. The Origin Crest is real, bounded, and active.
